## 📌 Project Overview

The **AWM Business Performance Dashboard** is a business analytics project focused on presenting business performance information through an organized and visual dashboard.

The project aims to make business data easier to understand by transforming raw information into meaningful visual representations, helping users explore performance patterns, monitor important metrics, and identify areas that may require attention.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze business performance using relevant data.
- Present important performance indicators in a clear format.
- Identify trends and patterns through visualizations.
- Make business information easier to interpret.
- Support informed, data-driven business decisions.
